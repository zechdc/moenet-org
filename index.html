Recent Project

Mobile Ordering for an 8-Location Coffee Chain

We helped a regional drive-thru coffee chain launch a branded mobile app integrated with Square POS, reaching their 48,000 loyalty members.

8 Locations
48k Loyalty Members
3 Vendors Evaluated

The Challenge

Speed up drive-thru orders without disrupting a workflow staff had mastered. The existing Square POS setup was fast and simple, and any changes had to respect that.

Our Approach

We evaluated three mobile app vendors on technical fit: API capabilities, Square Loyalty sync, catalog integration, and pricing models. Then designed a phased rollout to de-risk the transition.

The Outcome

A branded iOS and Android app with order-ahead, full Square Loyalty integration, and a workflow that lets customers order and pay before arriving at the window.

Why This Matters

Vendors talk features. We understand what matters to you: Will this work with my current setup? Will my staff actually use it? Will my loyalty members transfer over smoothly? Having built Square's platform for 5 years, we know what questions to ask and which promises are realistic. We help you avoid costly mistakes before you sign.
